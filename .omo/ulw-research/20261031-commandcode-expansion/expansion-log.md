## Saturation

The final countercheck sweep produced no new design-changing axis after the
scope reduction and gate assignments. Unanswered eligibility/production/live
behavior questions are not marked true or silently dropped; each has a named
stop gate in the executable plan. No unresolved lead is represented as verified
live behavior, numerical benefit or production support.

## Exclusions

No comparative performance benchmark, live model research-quality evaluation,
Rust stdio crash experiment, account login, remote hosted deployment or actual
third-party CLI redistribution was attempted. They belong to later authorized
implementation gates. BrowserSkill remained permission-blocked; public primary
HTTP sources and local code/command observations were used instead.
